

## Ideas
Extorted business owner - perhaps a casino owner?


## What was denied?
* Wealth
* Protection/safety


## Example
Toad
Cat 1
Name: Casino Royale

* What do you deserve that was denied to you?
	* My ticket to safety - my own profits!
* While you were starving, who was feasting?
	* Those greedy mafia bosses from the coast who just keep taking and taking and taking.
* Where do you draw the line?
	* I want to protect my employees. I won't take from them. The scum off the streets are fair game though

The host was a poor man who thought he made it big taking a loan from the mob to build up his casino. It was all chummy at first, but the protection cut grew quicker than the revenue, leaving the casino starving while the mob profited. We wasn't going to be able to make his next month, so in his desperation and desire a Toad sin developed. The sin ate a little extra cash from his guests, cut a little more out of the deal and gave him the buffer he needed to keep growing. The toad, of course, always wants more.
Cain was notified when people began to pick up that not everyone that goes into the casino leaves. 

The host now sits holed up in the vault of his casino, growing ever more reclusive and relying on the toad to bring him money, food, and security from the casino guests, building him the Palace he thinks he deserves
