# City of Sin
Sins grow from the roiling psychic sea, quickening in the psychically sensitive in response to trauma and ballooning into monsters. While this field runs through all humans and therefore the whole world, there are some places that it sits heavier, more like a fog from the sea than a morning mist. At its densest and most volatile, you reach places like the City of Sin, a god-forsaken monument to hubris standing in neon defiance to the desert around it. 

Some say something rotten sits at the heart of the City, twisting and darkening the psychic sea around it. Some within CAIN whisper that a great Lord has captured the space in its palace as some kind of twisted fantasy. Many believe that this is just what happens when you cram enough people into a place like this. The trust of the matter does not concern you. Your job as an exorcist does not change.

WIPE OUT THE STAIN.


## Design Goals
CAIN: City of Sin is designed to be a setting guide to help spark ideas for hunts and to make writing CAIN investigations easier. You could use it for a one-shot, a single hunt in a campaign, or maybe even set a whole game in it!
